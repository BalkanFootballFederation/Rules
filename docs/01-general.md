# 01 · General Regulations

> **Purpose:** Establish the standards expected of everyone taking part in BFF.

## 1.1 · Membership & conduct

Every player, club owner, manager, referee, and staff member must act respectfully and professionally during federation activities. Racism, discrimination, harassment, bullying, threats, doxxing, hate speech, and targeted abuse are prohibited.

Exploiting bugs, cheating, manipulating game mechanics, using unauthorized modifications, and impersonating federation officials are not allowed. Members must also comply with [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards).

## 1.2 · Fair competition

Match fixing, deliberate result manipulation, falsified reports, false registrations, and fabricated evidence undermine fair play and are prohibited.

All information submitted to federation officials must be accurate. Members may report concerns and provide recordings, screenshots, or other verifiable evidence.

## 1.3 · Official authority

**Division Staff or higher-ranking authorized officials** may review matters affecting official matches and competitions. The **League Staff Department** oversees administrative matters such as contracts, club locks, and ownership.

All reviews should be based on the applicable rules and available evidence.

## 1.4 · Communication

Official requests and disputes must be handled respectfully through recognized BFF channels. Clubs should appoint a clear representative to communicate with match officials and federation staff.
