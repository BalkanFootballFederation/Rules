# 05 · Players, Contracts & Transfers

> **Roster cap:** 15 registered players per club. **Contract cap:** 10 seasons.

## 5.1 · Squad registration

A club may have up to **15 officially registered players**. Player-owners are included when they are registered to play.

An additional player cannot be registered while the roster is full. A player must first be removed or transferred in accordance with the league registration process.

## 5.2 · Match eligibility

All players must be registered with the club they represent **before** an official match. Playing an unregistered or otherwise ineligible player may lead to an administrative review and a corrected match result.

## 5.3 · Contract duration

No player contract may exceed **10 seasons**. Contractual agreements must be accurately recorded and honored. Disputes should be referred to the **League Staff Department**.

## 5.4 · Club-lock review

**The League Staff Department must review club-lock cases.** A club lock cannot be used to bypass federation rules or impose an unjustified restriction. The department evaluates the facts and decides whether the lock is valid.

## 5.5 · Transfer Funds (TF)

Transfer Funds are BFF's transfer-related unit. The stated conversion is:

<div align="center">

### 2 TF = 1 Robux

</div>

TF can be purchased through **official BFF channels using transactions permitted by Roblox**. Only confirmed transactions are recognized. Falsifying balances or manipulating the TF system is prohibited.

## 5.6 · Transfer compliance

A transfer must respect player registration, current contract rules, and the **15-player roster cap**. Transfers suspected of being fraudulent may be investigated by **Division Staff or higher**.
