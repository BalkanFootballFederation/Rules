# 02 · Match Regulations

> **Core format:** 4v4 · 20 minutes · 2 players minimum per team.

## 2.1 · Team format

An official BFF fixture is played **4 versus 4**, with no more than four players on the pitch for either team. All players must be eligible and registered.

Each side must provide at least **two eligible players** to begin and continue the match.

## 2.2 · Match clock & deciding a winner

| Phase | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Regular match | **20 minutes** |
| If a winner is required and the score is tied | **Two extra periods of 5 minutes each** |
| If the score is still tied | **4 penalty kicks per team** |
| If still tied after those penalties | **Sudden death** until a winner is determined |

For competitions where draws are permitted, an equal score after regular time is a draw unless the specific competition rules say otherwise.

## 2.3 · Minimum players & forfeit results

If a club cannot field at least **two eligible players**, the opponent is awarded a **3–0 forfeit win**.

If a team falls below the minimum during play and cannot restore it, the referee must stop the match. If both clubs fail to meet the minimum, Division Staff decides the administrative outcome.

## 2.4 · Referee decisions

The authorized referee controls the match, enforces the playing rules, and records match incidents.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **There are no replays for referee mistakes.** A disputed call, missed foul, or incorrect decision does not by itself invalidate the result.

Suspected manipulation or serious rule breaches may still be investigated by **Division Staff or higher-ranking authorized officials**.

## 2.5 · Stadium suitability

Before kickoff, **the referee decides whether the stadium is fit for play**. Serious technical issues, unsuitable conditions, or unfair playing circumstances can justify delaying the start.

## 2.6 · Warm-ups

Teams have a maximum **one-hour warm-up period** before a match. The warm-up does not extend the agreed kickoff time without authorization.
