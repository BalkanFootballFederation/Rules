# 08 · Staff Authority & Investigations

> **Investigation threshold:** Division Staff or any higher-ranking authorized official.

## 8.1 · Division Staff powers

**Division Staff and all higher-ranking authorized officials** may investigate match results, forfeits, eligibility, scheduling, transfers, club activity, referee conduct, and other operational disputes.

Staff may request relevant evidence, review recordings and official records, and gather statements from the involved parties.

## 8.2 · Reviewing match results

Where there is a credible allegation of result manipulation, unregistered players, inaccurate reports, or a substantial regulatory breach, Division Staff or higher officials may investigate the fixture.

Following a review, authorized officials may correct official records or apply an administrative forfeit when justified by the competition rules.

**An ordinary referee mistake does not permit a match replay.**

## 8.3 · League Staff Department

The **League Staff Department** oversees club locks, contract disputes, ownership changes, and league administration. It may coordinate with Division Staff when cases involve both match and club matters.

## 8.4 · Staff standards

Staff must apply rules fairly and avoid conflicts of interest. Misuse of authority to benefit a club, manipulate a match, or interfere unfairly with the competition is prohibited.

## 8.5 · Decision records

Significant administrative decisions should identify the issue reviewed, evidence considered, relevant rules, and outcome. This helps maintain consistency and accountability.
