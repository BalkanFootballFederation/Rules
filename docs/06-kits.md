# 06 · Kits & Captain Identification

> **Kit principle:** opposing teams must be visually distinguishable.

## 6.1 · Acceptable kits

All clubs must wear appropriate, recognizable football kits. Kit designs must not contain racist, discriminatory, offensive, or otherwise inappropriate material.

BFF officials may require a club to change a kit that does not comply with these standards.

## 6.2 · Home and away conflicts

**The away team is responsible for changing its kit** if the home and away designs are identical or confusingly similar.

The referee may delay kickoff until the teams are sufficiently distinguishable on the pitch.

## 6.3 · Captains

Each team may appoint a captain. A captain **may wear an identifying armband**. The armband must meet the same content standards as the rest of the kit.

Captains should communicate respectfully with referees and team representatives.
