# 07 · Refereeing Standards

> **On-pitch authority:** the appointed referee is responsible for the conduct and control of the match.

## 7.1 · Referee duties

The referee must act impartially, apply match rules consistently, verify the minimum player count, identify potential kit conflicts, assess stadium suitability, and report relevant match incidents.

## 7.2 · Stadium inspection

**Only the appointed referee decides whether a stadium is suitable for kickoff** under BFF match requirements. Clubs must respect that decision.

## 7.3 · Match reporting

An accurate match report must be submitted when required. It should include the final score and relevant events. Knowingly falsifying an official report is prohibited.

## 7.4 · Disputed decisions

> [!NOTE]
> **There are no match replays for referee mistakes.**

Division Staff or higher-ranking officials may investigate suspected intentional misconduct, result manipulation, or serious procedural violations, but a routine refereeing mistake does not create a replay entitlement.

## 7.5 · Independence

Referees must not deliberately favor a club or player. Any conflict of interest or credible allegation of interference should be referred to authorized federation staff.
