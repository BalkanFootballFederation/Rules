# 09 · Final Provisions

## 9.1 · Who is covered?

These regulations apply to **all BFF players, club owners, managers, captains, referees, and staff** participating in official federation activities.

## 9.2 · Reading and following the rules

Members are responsible for knowing the requirements relevant to their role. Failure to read the rulebook does not exempt a participant from those requirements.

## 9.3 · Matters not expressly addressed

Questions not covered by a specific provision may be considered by the appropriate BFF department. Decisions should be consistent with existing regulations, supported by evidence, and communicated through official channels.

## 9.4 · Changes to the rulebook

BFF Management may publish amendments when necessary. Officially announced updates take effect according to the published notice.

The most recent official version in this repository is the applicable reference unless BFF Management states otherwise.
