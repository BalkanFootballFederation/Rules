# 04 · Clubs & Ownership

> **Owner commitment:** at least 30 days before voluntary resignation.

## 4.1 · Recognized ownership

Every registered BFF club must have a recognized owner. Owners are responsible for club administration, squad accuracy, match readiness, and cooperation with federation officials.

## 4.2 · Owners participating as players

**Club owners may play for their own clubs**, provided they are **officially registered as players** before taking part in a match.

Registered player-owners count toward the club's **15-player maximum** and must meet the same eligibility rules as any other player.

## 4.3 · Resignation requirements

An owner must remain in their position for at least **30 days** before requesting voluntary resignation.

After the 30-day minimum, the owner may file a formal resignation. Ownership changes and transfers must be reviewed and authorized by the **League Staff Department**.

BFF Management may replace an owner earlier in exceptional administrative circumstances.

## 4.4 · Day-to-day responsibilities

Owners must maintain accurate player registration, follow fixture arrangements, respect contract rules, and communicate through authorized channels.
