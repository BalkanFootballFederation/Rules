# 03 · Scheduling & Rescheduling

> **Key deadlines:** 24 hours for ordinary scheduling changes; 12 hours for exceptional forced rescheduling.

## 3.1 · Scheduling a fixture

All official matches must be scheduled at least **24 hours before kickoff**. Both clubs must be notified of the confirmed time and date, and their representatives must inform their players.

## 3.2 · Normal reschedule requests

A normal reschedule request must be filed at least **24 hours before the originally scheduled kickoff**. The new time is not valid until approved by the appropriate league officials.

Neither club can independently move an official fixture.

## 3.3 · Forced rescheduling

Forced rescheduling is reserved for exceptional cases with a valid reason. Requests must arrive at least **12 hours before the original kickoff**, and **Division Staff or higher** must approve them.

A request made outside the permitted notice period may be declined.

## 3.4 · Fixture attendance

Each club must be ready with at least two eligible players. Failure to meet that requirement results in a **3–0 forfeit defeat**, subject to investigation if both sides fail the requirement or exceptional circumstances arise.

## 3.5 · Quick reference

| Action | Minimum notice | Who must authorize it? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Schedule a match | **24 hours** | Official competition scheduling |
| Normal reschedule | **24 hours** | Appropriate league officials |
| Forced reschedule | **12 hours** | **Division Staff or higher** |
